# Privacy Policy for Apple TV Remote

**Last updated: May 2026**

## Data Collection

Apple TV Remote does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

- **No analytics**: No usage tracking, no crash reporting, no analytics SDKs.
- **No network communication**: The app communicates only with Apple TV devices on your local network. No data is sent to external servers.
- **No account required**: No sign-in, no registration, no user accounts.
- **Local storage only**: Pairing credentials are stored locally in `~/.appletv_control/` and never leave your device.

## Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.
